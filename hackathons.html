Winner · Sustainable Development Ideathon 2025

Freight matching for Indian logistics

Indian logistics includes fragmented truck capacity, disconnected shipment information, empty return journeys and difficult coordination across transport modes. Our team proposed a ride-hailing-style platform to match compatible loads with available trucks.

We considered route planning and documentation alongside matching. The concept was informed by the direction of the National Logistics Policy and Unified Logistics Interface Platform, both intended to improve coordination across stakeholders.

Available truck+Compatible load→Match & route→More useful fleet time

Winner · Agriculture Hackathon 2025

Water prediction for vertical farming

Vertical farming needs precise irrigation and micronutrient delivery. Our team designed an improved irrigation arrangement and paired it with predictive modeling to help evaluate water usage.

I trained the model for the team's testing workflow and water-use prediction. The combined physical design and model let us examine the irrigation concept as a system.
