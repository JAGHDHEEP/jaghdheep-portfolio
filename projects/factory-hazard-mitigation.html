Factory Hazard Mitigation

A real-time worker–machine proximity system that connects YOLOv8 object detection, ultrasonic distance readings and immediate alerts.

The problem

On a busy factory floor, a worker may approach a machine without noticing the danger in time. Vision alone can be affected by occlusion, lighting and camera angle. I designed the monitor to cross-check visual detections with a physical distance reading.

System design

YOLOv8 detects people and machines in the camera feed while OpenCV handles image processing. Ultrasonic sensors provide distance measurements. The system compares these signals against a safety threshold and passes a breach to a microcontroller-driven alert.

Camera+Ultrasonic sensor→Proximity check→Worker alert

What I contributed

  • Integrated real-time object detection with sensor readings for the proximity decision.
  • Connected the threshold logic to immediate hands-free alerts.
  • Designed around the noisy, changing conditions that make a single sensor less reliable.

Outcome

The prototype demonstrates a complete loop from perception to physical warning. It brings computer vision and embedded sensing into one safety workflow. I would test false alert rates, missed detections and latency across different lighting and factory layouts before operational use.
